~ ~ ~   PiXtionApps   ~ ~ ~

All apps need the ofxOpenNI2Grabber addon (https://github.com/n1ckfg/ofxOpenNI2Grabber) in openFrameworks/addons.

1. PiXtion_Analog
Draws depth and color maps onscreen at a fixed 720x480 layout, for analog video out.

2. PiXtion_Basic
Draws depth and color maps onscreen.

3. PiXtion_Calibration
Streams uncompressed real-world position and color.

4. PiXtion_Compression
Streams compressed real-world position and color.
Needs the ofxZip addon (https://github.com/n1ckfg/ofxZip); view with tools/PiCompressionViewer.

5. PiXtion_Contour
Streams depth as contours.

6. PiXtion_Contour_Viz
Draws IR as contours onscreen.

7. PiXtion_Glitch
Draws depth as colored contours fullscreen; set do_osc_contours to also stream them.

8. PiXtion_IR
Draws the IR map fullscreen.

9. PiXtion_Osc
Streams compressed depth and color maps.

10. PiXtion_Scanline
Streams depth as horizontal lines.

11. PiXtion_Scanline_Viz
Draws depth as horizontal lines in 3D onscreen; drag to orbit.
